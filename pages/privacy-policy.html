What we collect and why

  • Orders: your name, email, shipping address and order details (items, colors). We use them to process, ship and support your order, and to meet our accounting obligations. Legal basis: performance of the contract and legal obligations.
  • Payment: payments are handled by our payment provider Whop, inside a secure checkout window on our page. We never see or store your full card details. Whop processes your data under its own privacy policy.
  • Delivery estimate: when you visit the page, your approximate city is estimated from your IP address through the service geojs.io, only to show a delivery date. This estimate is not stored by us.
  • Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Contact: when you email us, we keep your messages to answer you.

Cookies and storage

Our page does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It only keeps a small note in your browser for the current visit (for example, that you closed a pop-up). The Whop checkout may use the cookies it needs to process your payment securely.

How long we keep data

Order data is kept for as long as needed for the order and support, then for 10 years for accounting records as required by law. Contact messages are kept for up to 3 years after our last exchange.

Who receives your data

Only the providers that help us run the shop: our payment provider (Whop), our shipping carriers, and our hosting provider. We never sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or limit its use, by emailing contact@coocoat.com. You can also file a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.
