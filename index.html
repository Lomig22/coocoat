Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a power bank? Is one included?

Coocoat runs on any standard 5V USB power bank, which is not included. Use one you already have, or tick the Power Bank add-on above the Add to cart button. No wall outlet needed: stay warm on the sofa, at your desk, in the stands or on a chilly walk.

When will I get my order?

Delivery is free and tracked, within 7 days. You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

What if I don't love it?

You're covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee. If it's not for you, request a return within 30 days of delivery under our refund policy.

How fast does it warm up?

Fast. Switch it on and the heating panel spreads warmth across your back and shoulders, right where you feel the cold most, while the plush fleece lining holds it in.

How long does the warmth last?

It depends on the power bank you plug in: the bigger its capacity, the longer you stay warm.

Will it fit me?

Yes. It's one size: 150 × 80 cm, with a relaxed, oversized cape cut that fits a wide range of body types. The zip-front adds structure without feeling tight and leaves plenty of room to move.

Is it safe?

It runs on 5V low voltage from a USB power bank, the same kind of power you use to charge a phone.

Is it machine washable?

Yes. Remove the power bank and unclip the detachable power button, then machine wash following the care label. Let it air dry completely before reconnecting, and don't iron over the heating panel.

Yes! The Duo Bundle gets you 2 Coocoats for €70 instead of €89.90, and you pick each color. Perfect for gifting.
